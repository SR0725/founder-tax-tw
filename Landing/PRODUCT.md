# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders who live in Taiwan and own a US company, usually a Delaware LLC (single- or multi-member) or a C-Corp. Today they track things in a spreadsheet and pay a US accountant at year end. Nobody handles the Taiwan-side tax effects.

The audience for this landing page tonight: YC founder judges at the c0mpiled-18 hackathon (Taipei, 2026-11-11), plus the 90-second demo video.

## Job

Tell the founder which US filings their company owes, when each is due and what missing it costs. Then produce draft filings with uncertain fields flagged, and a review checklist that a reviewer without a CPA license can work through before a licensed CPA signs.

Flow: upload Stripe export + bank statements → obligations, deadlines, penalties → AI-filled drafts with flagged fields → review checklist.

## Positioning

- Only for Taiwanese founders. Covers the US side and the Taiwan side (CFC, overseas income); competitors (doola, Firstbase) cover the US side only.
- Different cost structure: reviewers without a license do most of the work with an AI checklist. The CPA only signs.
- Maps to YC RFS "The $15-an-Hour Expert" (Winter 2027).

## Constraints

- "Not tax advice" is always visible on screen.
- Deadlines, penalties and thresholds come from a hard-coded rule table with official source links. The model never invents them. Unverified rules are labeled as drafts.
- No invented customers, testimonials, user counts, benchmarks or pricing.
- Product copy in English. Team communication in Traditional Chinese.
- Demo data is synthetic (fictional company: Lanyu Labs LLC).
